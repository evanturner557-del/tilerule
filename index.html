Questions

Common questions

How much wastage should I allow?

Around 10% for a straight lay is a common rule of thumb; allow more for diagonal or herringbone patterns.

Does it include grout gaps?

No. Gaps slightly reduce the count, so this errs on the safe side.

Is anything stored?

No. It runs in your browser.
